## Findings

### A-01 · Flow Survey can classify unequal time windows as current continuity evidence

**Severity High · Priority P1 · Verified in live Chromium and Firefox**

Imported two native-format FDV files through the source picker and a local workbook mapping FM01 upstream of FM02. Both monitors recorded a constant 0.1 m³/s for five one-minute samples. The initial live balance reported 24 m³ against 24 m³, ratio 1.000 and Green. Applying an approved synthetic exclusion to upstream flow alone over the first 120 s left 120 s of common support and 12 m³ on each side. The live result instead used downstream 24 m³ and upstream 12 m³, ratio 2.000 and Green with 100% support. It suggested possible lateral or unmonitored inflow despite identical data on the retained common support. Moving the same exclusion to downstream flow produced a 12/24 m³ ratio of 0.500 and a false Red with 100% support. Both engines returned the same values and decisions. The Volume balance page describes common valid temporal support. The implemented weekly method integrates each monitor on its own retained mask and then compares the independent totals. A path is considered complete when individual monitor statuses are complete. The shared validity model defines complete relative to each monitor's own eligible time after exclusions. A targeted exclusion can therefore change the volume ratio and RAG even if the valid hydraulics are identical whenever both stations are measured. The combined row's coverage is the minimum individual fraction, which can still be 100%. [Chromium survey observations](observations-chromium-survey.json) · [Firefox survey observations](observations-firefox-survey.json) · [Survey reproduction](survey-observation.mjs) · [Upstream FDV](FM01.FDV) · [Downstream FDV](FM02.FDV) · [Association workbook](A-TECH-survey-associations.xlsx) · [Volume balance loop](https://github.com/anzarsajid91/ICM_Graphing_Tool/blob/ccc36427c12f003e5d9b1fee61632770b31952cc/src/icm_workbench/analysis/survey_context.py#L743) · [Validity contract](https://github.com/anzarsajid91/ICM_Graphing_Tool/blob/ccc36427c12f003e5d9b1fee61632770b31952cc/src/icm_workbench/analysis/validity.py#L36) · [User-facing volume balance scope](https://github.com/anzarsajid91/ICM_Graphing_Tool/blob/ccc36427c12f003e5d9b1fee61632770b31952cc/web/assets/precision-workbench.js#L107)

**Acceptance** — For each path/week, integrate the downstream and every required upstream monitor over the same union-of-exclusions-adjusted intersection of valid intervals. Retain individual source coverage and separately show common seconds, full requested seconds and support/eligibility status. With 0.1 m³/s constant at both stations for 240 s and an upstream-only 120 s exclusion, the matching support is 120 s, 12 m³ per station and a diagnostic ratio of 1.0. If policy withholds a RAG on partial requested support, explicitly mark it insufficient. Test downstream-only and disjoint source exclusions, incomplete downstream coverage, multiple tributaries, unequal timesteps, exact week boundaries and re-opened evidence. Keep the legacy signed FSAT classification separately labelled and review impact on the schematic, monthly evidence, reports and workspace restoration.

### A-03 · Missing one Detriment spill statistic hides supported detriment in the other

**Severity High · Priority P1 · Verified in live Chromium and Firefox**

With zero tolerance and either criterion selected, `COUNT` had 2 → 5 spills (+3) with missing proposed duration. `DURATION` rose from 1 to 3.25 h (+2.25) with missing proposed count. The live table displayed the valid differences but assigned both criterion states and both overall rows **Unavailable**. A complete companion row with the same two differences was correctly Detriment. The summary consequently showed one detriment and two unresolved rows. The code assigns both criteria to unavailable whenever either delta is unavailable. This can hide supported adverse evidence in rollups and hotspot selection. [Baseline fixture](A-TECH-spill-baseline.csv) · [Proposed fixture](A-TECH-spill-proposed.csv) · [Local audited result](chromium-detriment-export.csv) · [Joint availability branch](https://github.com/anzarsajid91/ICM_Graphing_Tool/blob/ccc36427c12f003e5d9b1fee61632770b31952cc/src/icm_workbench/detriment_api.py#L434)

**Acceptance** — Evaluate count and duration independently whenever the corresponding two valid inputs and matched basis exist. `COUNT` shows count Detriment +3 and duration Unavailable, while `DURATION` shows duration Detriment +2.25 h and count Unavailable. Under either criterion, rollups, filtered views, eligible hotspot evidence and CSV/HTML retain the proven detriment plus an explicit incomplete-criterion marker. When the only supported criterion is unchanged, improved or within tolerance, the combined result must retain unresolved status for the absent criterion and avoid implying full clearance. Define and separately test count-primary and legacy policies, one missing input on either side, unmatched assets, tolerance boundaries and complete comparisons. Retain the raw report row and confirmed scope.
