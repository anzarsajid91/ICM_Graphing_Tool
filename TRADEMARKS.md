# HydraBench name and logo

HydraBench is the name of the official project maintained by original creator
Anzar Sajid. Its logo is the charcoal and indigo hydraulic-path H with rounded
junctions and the HydraBench wordmark. The official upstream source is the
[HydraBench repository](https://github.com/anzarsajid91/ICM_Graphing_Tool).

Contributors, reviewers and distributors may identify HydraBench by name
and accurately describe where their work originates. Label third-party forks,
builds and distributions clearly so readers can distinguish them from release
artifacts published by the official project. Avoid implying upstream approval,
endorsement or affiliation without permission.

This policy reserves applicable rights in the name and logo. It makes no
claim of trademark registration. The [software licence](LICENSE) governs
software modification and distribution. This policy adds no restriction to
those permissions and preserves truthful attribution and lawful reference use.
